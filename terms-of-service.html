2. Description of Service

FocusForge is a productivity and focus management application designed specifically for people with ADHD. The App provides:

2.1 Core Features (Free)

  • Focus Sessions: Timer-based focus sessions with momentum tracking
  • Friction (Impulse Control): Countdown timers before opening distracting apps
  • App Blocking: Block distracting apps during focus sessions
  • Task Management: ADHD-friendly task tracking without shame mechanics
  • Daily Routines: Structured routines for morning, evening, and work
  • Basic Insights: View your focus patterns and progress
  • Energy Logging: Track your energy levels throughout the day

2.2 Pro Features (Subscription Required)

  • Advanced Insights: Sleep-focus correlations, peak hours, day patterns
  • Unlimited Routines: Create custom routines for any scenario
  • Schedule-Based Blocking: Automatic blocking based on your schedule
  • Advanced Friction Settings: Customizable delays and rules
  • Priority Support: Faster response times for help requests
  • Early Access: Try new features before general release
