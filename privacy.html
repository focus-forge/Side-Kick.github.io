🔒 Privacy First: SideKick stores all your data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal information.

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SideKick ("we," "our," or "the app") handles your data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

Data We Access

SideKick accesses the following types of data solely for app functionality:

1. Screen Time Data (via Screen Time API)

  • What: Information about which apps you've selected to block
  • Why: To apply friction shields and focus schedules
  • Storage: Stored locally on your device only
  • Sharing: Never shared with anyone, including us

2. Health Data (via Apple HealthKit)

  • What: Sleep analysis data, including bedtime, wake time, total sleep duration, and sleep stages
  • Why: To provide personalized focus insights based on how your rest patterns affect your productivity and impulse control
  • Storage: Stored locally on your device only. Health data is never written back to HealthKit.
  • Sharing: Never leaves your device, is never transmitted to any server, and is never shared with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers
  • Optional: HealthKit access is entirely optional. You can deny or revoke access at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Health. All core features (app blocking, friction shields, focus schedules, task management) work without HealthKit.
  • No Marketing Use: Health data is never used for advertising, marketing, or any purpose other than displaying sleep-focus insights within the app

3. App Usage Patterns

  • What: How many times you've opened blocked apps, session durations
  • Why: To adjust adaptive friction and show usage insights
  • Storage: Stored locally on your device only
  • Sharing: Never transmitted off your device

Data We Do NOT Collect

We want to be clear about what we do NOT do:

  • ❌ No account creation or login required
  • ❌ No cloud storage or syncing
  • ❌ No analytics or tracking
  • ❌ No third-party data sharing
  • ❌ No advertisements or ad tracking
  • ❌ No location tracking
  • ❌ No contact information collection
  • ❌ No screenshots or screen recordings
  • ❌ No server-side logging

How We Use Your Data

All data is used exclusively on your device for these purposes:

  • Applying friction shields when you try to open blocked apps
  • Managing focus schedules and time-based blocking
  • Calculating adaptive friction based on your usage patterns
  • Displaying sleep-based focus insights
  • Showing your progress and statistics in the app
Important: Because all data stays on your device, if you delete the app, all your data is permanently deleted with it.

In-App Purchases

SideKick offers optional in-app purchases (subscriptions) to unlock Pro features. Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information.

Apple may collect:

  • Purchase history
  • Payment method information
  • Billing address

Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for information on how Apple handles this data.

Third-Party Services

SideKick uses Apple's native frameworks only:

  • Screen Time API (Family Controls) - For app blocking
  • HealthKit - For sleep data (optional)
  • WidgetKit - For home screen widgets
  • StoreKit - For in-app purchases

We do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data Retention

Because all data is stored locally on your device, data retention is entirely under your control:

  • Active Use: Data is retained on your device as long as the app is installed
  • Deletion: Deleting the app permanently removes all associated data
  • In-App Deletion: You can delete all data from within the app via Settings > Data & Privacy > Delete All Data
  • Export: You can export your data via Settings > Data & Privacy > Export My Data before deleting

We do not retain any user data on servers, as no data is ever transmitted from your device.

Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: All your data is viewable within the app at any time
  • Deletion: Delete all data from within the app or by uninstalling
  • Export: Export your data in JSON format from the app's Settings
  • Control: Revoke HealthKit or Screen Time access at any time in iOS Settings
  • Consent Withdrawal: Stop using the app at any time; uninstalling removes all data

For Users in the European Economic Area (GDPR)

Because SideKick stores all data locally on your device and does not collect, process, or transmit personal data to any server, the traditional GDPR data controller/processor framework does not apply in the conventional sense. However, we respect the spirit of GDPR and affirm the following rights:

  • Right of Access: All data is visible within the app
  • Right to Rectification: You can modify your settings and data within the app
  • Right to Erasure: Delete all data via Settings or by uninstalling the app
  • Right to Data Portability: Export your data in a machine-readable format (JSON) from Settings
  • Right to Object: You can stop using any feature at any time, including revoking HealthKit or Screen Time permissions

For any GDPR-related inquiries, please contact us using the information below.

For Users in California (CCPA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have specific rights regarding their personal information. Because SideKick does not collect, sell, or share personal information with third parties, the following applies:

  • Right to Know: We do not collect personal information beyond what is stored locally on your device
  • Right to Delete: You can delete all data via the app's Settings or by uninstalling
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale: We do not sell personal information to any third party
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We do not discriminate against users who exercise their privacy rights

Children's Privacy

SideKick is designed for individuals aged 13 and older who manage their own devices. The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has installed the app, please uninstall it from their device. Since all data is stored locally, uninstalling the app removes all associated data. You may also contact us for assistance.

Users between 13 and 17 should review these terms with a parent or guardian before using the app.

Data Breach Notification

Because SideKick stores all data locally on your device and does not transmit data to any server, the risk of a data breach through our services is minimal. We do not maintain databases of user information.

In the unlikely event that a vulnerability in the app could compromise locally stored data, we will promptly notify affected users through an app update and post details on this page.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
