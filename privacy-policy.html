Last Updated: January 22, 2026

Our Privacy Promise

We understand that managing ADHD is personal. Your focus patterns, energy levels, sleep data, and daily struggles are deeply private. That's why SideKick is built with a local-first architecture — most of your data never leaves your device.

We don't sell your data. We don't use it for advertising. We don't track you across apps. Period.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored only on your device and never sent to our servers:

  • Focus session history and momentum scores
  • Task lists, routines, and schedules
  • Energy level logs and patterns
  • Sleep tracking data and correlations
  • Impulse control events (friction interactions)
  • App blocking preferences and settings
  • Personal insights and pattern analysis
  • Daily summaries and progress data

1.2 Health Data (HealthKit)

With your explicit permission, we access data from Apple HealthKit to help you understand how sleep and activity affect your focus:

Data Type Purpose
Sleep Analysis Correlate sleep quality with next-day focus patterns
Step Count Track activity levels and energy correlation
Active Energy Understand how physical activity affects productivity

Your HealthKit data:

  • Is stored locally on your device only
  • Never leaves your device or is sent to our servers
  • Is never shared with third parties
  • Is not used for advertising or marketing
  • Is not sold or disclosed to data brokers
  • Remains under your complete control

1.3 Screen Time Permissions

SideKick uses Apple's Screen Time API (FamilyControls framework) to enable app blocking features.

What we CAN do:

  • Block or shield apps you select during focus sessions
  • Apply Friction (countdown timers before opening distracting apps)
  • Restrict app access during scheduled routines

What we CANNOT do:

  • See which apps you use or when
  • Track how much time you spend in apps
  • Access your device usage history
  • Monitor your browsing activity
  • View your Screen Time statistics

1.4 Subscription Data

When you subscribe to SideKick Pro through the App Store, Apple shares limited information with us through our subscription management service (Superwall):

  • Anonymous Subscriber ID (unique to you and our app, NOT your Apple ID)
  • Subscription status (active, expired, or cancelled)
  • Purchase date and renewal date
  • Your country or region (for tax purposes)
  • Device type and iOS version

We do NOT receive: Your name, email address, Apple ID, payment card information, or billing address.

1.5 Technical Data

We collect basic technical information to ensure the app works properly:

  • Device model and iOS version
  • App version number
  • Crash logs and error reports
  • App performance metrics

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Providing App Functionality

  • Enable app blocking and friction features
  • Display your focus momentum and insights
  • Show sleep-focus correlations and patterns
  • Save your routines, tasks, and preferences
  • Provide personalized recommendations
  • Unlock Pro features when you subscribe

2.2 Improving the App

  • Fix bugs and crashes
  • Optimize performance and battery usage
  • Develop new features based on user needs
  • Ensure compatibility with new iOS versions

2.3 What We Do NOT Use Your Data For

  • Advertising or marketing to you or others
  • Selling to third parties or data brokers
  • Tracking across other apps or websites
  • Building user profiles for unrelated purposes
  • Behavioral targeting or profiling
  • Sharing with social media platforms

3. Data Storage & Security

3.1 Local Storage (Primary)

Most of your data is stored locally on your device using SwiftData with encryption at rest. This means:

  • Your data never leaves your device
  • Complete control over your information
  • No server breaches can expose your data
  • Instant data deletion when you delete the app

3.2 iCloud Sync (Optional)

If you enable iCloud sync:

  • Your app data is backed up to your iCloud account
  • Data syncs across your devices with the same Apple ID
  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple
  • Only you can access this data through your Apple ID
  • We cannot access your iCloud data

3.3 Third-Party Storage

Superwall (AWS, United States):

  • Subscription status and purchase events
  • Paywall interaction data
  • Anonymous user identifiers
  • Device technical information

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest.

3.4 Security Measures

  • Encryption in transit (TLS/HTTPS)
  • Encryption at rest for stored data
  • Secure coding practices following Apple's guidelines
  • Regular security reviews and updates
  • Limited data collection (only what's necessary)

4. Sharing Your Information

We do NOT sell your personal information to anyone.

4.1 Service Providers

  • Apple: App Store purchases, iCloud sync, HealthKit, Screen Time API
  • Superwall: Subscription management and paywall optimization

4.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, to respond to legal requests, or to protect rights and safety.

4.3 What We Do NOT Share With

  • Advertisers or advertising networks
  • Data brokers or data aggregators
  • Social media platforms
  • Analytics companies for tracking

5. Your Rights & Choices

5.1 Access Your Data

View all data stored by the app in Settings → Privacy → My Data, or email us for a complete export.

5.2 Delete Your Data

Three options:

  1. Delete the App: Removes all local data immediately
  2. In-App Deletion: Settings → Privacy → Delete All Data
  3. Email Request: Contact us at caazamar@gmail.com

5.3 Revoke Permissions

  • HealthKit: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → SideKick
  • Screen Time: iOS Settings → Screen Time → SideKick → Remove
  • Notifications: iOS Settings → Notifications → SideKick

5.4 Manage Subscription

iOS Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → SideKick

5.5 For EU Users (GDPR)

You have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, data portability, object to processing, and withdraw consent. Contact us at caazamar@gmail.com to exercise these rights.

5.6 For California Users (CCPA/CPRA)

You have rights to know, delete, correct, opt-out of sale (we don't sell data), and non-discrimination. Contact us at caazamar@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy

SideKick is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will promptly delete it.
